Marisol Heat is an original top-down open-world crime game in vanilla JavaScript on one canvas - no libraries, no assets, nothing taken from any existing game. The city of Marisol is generated from a seed: avenue grid, downtown towers, old town, suburbs, docks, a park, a beach and the sea, with named places (Marisol PD HQ, St. Solana Hospital, Bayside Respray, Iron & Oak Guns, QuickMart 24/7, Club Marisol, Pier 9 Warehouse, Venn Motors, Calle Ocho Hideout, Casa Del Mar). You walk and drive anywhere, steal any car, and live traffic and pedestrians react around you. Crimes raise a five-star wanted level; police hunt in packs, ram, shoot at three stars and up, and bust you if they box you in. Work through four story jobs (a stolen coupe to deliver, a timed package run, a register robbery with a clean getaway, an accountant to escort), then keep earning with taxi fares and repeatable shop robberies in free roam. Fists or a purchasable pistol, health and armour pickups, a respray shop that clears your heat, a day-night clock, a live minimap, and progress that saves to localStorage.

Controls: WASD or arrows to move and drive, E to enter or exit a car, F (or Space) to punch or shoot, Shift to run, P or Esc to pause.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. The game is the `game/` folder - plain static files, fully offline. `node main.ts` is the entry program that validates the screenshots and builds the submission dossier.
